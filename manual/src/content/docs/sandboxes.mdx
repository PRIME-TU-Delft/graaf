A sandbox is a private space for graphs that don't belong to a course. Use it to experiment,
prototype a graph before proposing it for a course, or build something for a one-off assignment.

<Aside type="caution">
	A sandbox is **not** a course. Its content shouldn't be posted publicly, and sandboxes are prone
	to being lost or deleted over time. If you're building something for an actual university course,
	ask a programme administrator to set up a course instead. See the [Programmes](../programmes)
	page.
</Aside>

## How sandboxes differ from courses

- No programme hierarchy: a sandbox belongs to one person (its **Owner**), not a programme.
- Simpler roles: **Owner** and **Editor**, instead of a course's Admin/Editor split. See
  [Sandbox roles](#sandbox-roles) below.
- A sandbox can be deleted by its owner at any time, with no archive step.
- Creating graphs, editing domains/subjects/lectures, and everything else about working inside a
  graph works exactly the same as in a course. See the [Graphs](../graphs),
  [Domains](../domains), [Subjects](../subjects), and [Lectures](../lectures) pages for how-tos on
  that.

<Aside type="note">
	Sharing a sandbox graph via a public link isn't available yet, unlike course graphs (see
	[Links](../links)). This is planned but not yet implemented.
</Aside>

name, and you're the owner of a new, empty sandbox.

## Viewing a sandbox

Opening a sandbox card works the same as opening a course card on the [Courses](../courses) page:
click "View" (or "View/Edit" if you're the owner or an editor) to reach its overview page, listing
the sandbox's graphs. A sandbox has no pinned/archived grouping and no "request access" fallback,
since only the owner and editors can see it at all.

## Sandbox roles

- **Owner** can rename the sandbox, add or remove editors, transfer ownership, and delete the
  sandbox. A sandbox has exactly one owner.
- **Editor** can create and edit graphs in the sandbox, but cannot change its settings, add other
  editors, or delete it.

Both owner and editors are shown as "Super Users" on a sandbox's card in the sandbox list.

## Sandbox settings

Only the owner can open a sandbox's settings page, reached via "Settings" on the sandbox overview
page.

{/* TODO: screenshot */}

### Renaming a sandbox

Click "Edit sandbox name" at the top of the settings page.

### Managing editors

Adding and removing editors uses the same "Add super users" dialog and table pattern as
[Courses](../courses#add-or-change-super-users). The differences: a sandbox has only one role
(editor, no admin), so there's no role picker, and removing an editor is a "Revoke" button in
their table row instead of a role-badge menu.

### Transferring ownership

In the danger zone at the bottom of the settings page, the owner can transfer ownership to another
editor. The current owner becomes an editor and immediately loses access to the settings page.
This cannot be undone by the person transferring ownership.

### Deleting a sandbox

Also in the danger zone, the owner can permanently delete the sandbox, along with all its graphs
and links. Deleting requires typing the sandbox's name to confirm, and cannot be undone.

## Leaving a sandbox

An editor (not the owner) can leave a sandbox from its overview page. This removes their editor
access; the owner or another editor needs to add them back for them to regain access.
